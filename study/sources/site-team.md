## Culture

We're a close-knit, flat team from OpenAI, Google Brain, Meta/FAIR, Stripe, Airbnb, Plaid, Docker, and more. We're backed by top-tier investors who share our vision for building the foundation of truly transformative AI.

Our team works in-person five days a week in our San Francisco office near the Embarcadero station. We offer competitive salaries, equity, and benefits, as well as lunch and dinner.

We love what we do and care about our work a lot. We strive for excellence and craftsmanship and won't stop until we get there. When the team wins, we all win, and we enjoy collaborating and inspiring each other to grow as a team and as individuals.

We want you to be able to do the most impactful work of your career at TypeSafe and help define our future as a company.

## Values

**Positive-Sum Games with Long-Term People** — When the team wins, we all win.

**Thinking in Bets** — Good decision-making involves a lot of uncertainty. We prioritize long-term goals over short-term gains.

**First Principles** — We want our decisions to always stem from clear reasoning, grounded in our values and culture.

**Prioritize Learning** — We recognize that a key to success is learning as fast as possible about ourselves, our team, our processes, our product, the market, and everything else involved with accomplishing our mission.

**Passion** — We love what we do. We're looking for people who share that passion and are similarly enthralled by the challenges we've taken on.

**A working environment built for human beings** — We believe that emotions are a core part of what makes us human, and what ultimately fuels our passion for the work we do. We build machines; we don't try to be machines.
